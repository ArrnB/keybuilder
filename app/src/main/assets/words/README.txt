words.txt is the word list behind KeyBuilder's suggestions and autocorrect.
Each line: word <TAB> score <TAB> language (e = English, h = Hinglish, b = both).
It is generated by tools/build_words.py.

Sources and credit
- English and Hindi word frequencies: the wordfreq project by Robyn Speer and contributors,
  https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq . wordfreq's data is licensed under the Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (CC BY-SA 4.0), and it lists its own sources there.
  words.txt is derived from that data and is offered under the same licence.
- Rare English words are kept only if they are also in the "web2" word list
  (Webster's Second International, 1934; public domain).
- About 800 Hinglish words and short forms were written by hand for this app.
